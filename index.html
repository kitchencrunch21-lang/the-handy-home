How it works

Three steps. Zero experience needed.

Every guide on this site follows the same beginner-proof format, so you always know what is coming next.

01

Pick your project

Choose a guide that matches the thing that is broken. Each one shows the time and difficulty up front, so you never start something you cannot finish.

02

Gather the basics

Every guide opens with a short tools list, mostly things you already own. Anything you need to buy costs a few dollars at any hardware store.

03

Follow the steps

Work through the numbered steps in order. We flag the common mistakes at each stage, so you learn what the pros watch out for.
