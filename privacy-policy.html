Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

The Handy Home ("we", "our") respects your privacy. This page explains what information this site collects and what happens to it. In short: very little, and nothing sneaky.

Information you share directly

If you use the contact form or sign up for the newsletter, we receive the details you type in: your name, email address, and message. That information is used only to reply to you or to send the newsletter you asked for. It is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, this site may collect basic technical data when you visit: your browser type, device type, pages viewed, and approximate location derived from your IP address. This is used in aggregate to understand which articles are useful, and it cannot identify you personally.

Cookies

This site uses a small number of cookies to remember basic preferences (such as dismissing a notice) and to measure anonymous traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

Third-party services

Analytics or newsletter tools used by this site process data on our behalf and are bound by their own privacy policies. We do not control those services, but we choose ones with clear privacy practices.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of any personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@thehandyhome.com and the request will be handled promptly.

Children

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
